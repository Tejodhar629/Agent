# Monetization Strategy

Seva Setu balances social impact (B2C) with sustainable revenue generation (B2B SaaS).

## 1. B2C Model: Free for Citizens
*   The core citizen experience (profiling, AI chat, scheme discovery, and application tracking) is **100% Free** and **Ad-Free**.
*   *Why?* To build a massive user base, establish trust, and democratize access to welfare. Our "product" at this layer is the data and lead generation potential.

## 2. B2B Model: SaaS for Consultants (CSCs, NGOs, Agents)
This is the primary revenue engine. Consultants use the platform as a CRM and workflow automation tool.

### Tiered Subscription Plans
*   **Basic (Freemium):** 
    *   Profile listing in the directory.
    *   Manage up to 10 offline citizen profiles.
    *   Basic access to scheme eligibility tools.
*   **Pro (e.g., ₹999/month):**
    *   Unlimited citizen profiles / CRM capabilities.
    *   **AI Auto-Fill:** Bulk verification and AI-assisted PDF report generation for their clients.
    *   Priority listing in the "Find a Consultant" marketplace (better visibility).
    *   WhatsApp integration (sending updates directly to their clients via Seva Setu).
*   **Enterprise (NGOs / Gram Panchayats):**
    *   Custom pricing. Multi-agent dashboards, detailed reporting, and bulk data exports.

## 3. Marketplace & Lead Generation Fees
*   **Matchmaking Commission:** If a citizen requests an offline consultation or document-filing assistance through the Seva Setu platform, we charge a small lead-generation fee (e.g., ₹50 - ₹100) to the consultant for the verified, highly-qualified lead.
*   **Escrow/Payments (Future):** Integrated payment gateways where citizens pay consultants for document processing via Seva Setu. The platform takes a 5% transaction fee.

## 4. Data Licensing & API Access (Phase 3)
*   **Anonymized Analytics:** Selling aggregated, anonymized demographic data reports (e.g., "Demand for agricultural schemes in Maharashtra") to policy think-tanks, researchers, or state governments.
*   **B2B APIs:** Offering our RAG Eligibility Engine as an API for banks, micro-finance institutions, and agricultural startups to assess credit-worthiness or cross-sell products based on scheme eligibility.
