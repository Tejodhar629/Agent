# Top Indian Government Schemes, Certificates, and Tax Services

This document outlines the most common and heavily utilized government services by Indian citizens. It serves as foundational data for the Seva Setu Platform's knowledge base and RAG pipeline.

## Top 10 Most Common Government Schemes

1. **Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)**
   - *Purpose:* National mission for financial inclusion to ensure access to financial services like banking, savings/deposit accounts, remittance, credit, insurance, and pension in an affordable manner.
2. **Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)**
   - *Purpose:* World's largest government-funded healthcare program targeting over 50 crore beneficiaries, providing a health cover of Rs. 5 lakhs per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
3. **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)**
   - *Purpose:* Income support scheme providing ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments to all landholding farmer families.
4. **Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)**
   - *Purpose:* Enhances livelihood security in rural areas by providing at least 100 days of guaranteed wage employment in a financial year to every household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.
5. **Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY - Urban/Gramin)**
   - *Purpose:* Initiative to provide affordable housing to the urban and rural poor with a target of building millions of affordable houses.
6. **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)**
   - *Purpose:* A government-backed savings scheme targeted at the parents of girl children, encouraging them to build a fund for the future education and marriage expenses of their female child.
7. **Atal Pension Yojana (APY)**
   - *Purpose:* Pension scheme focused on the unorganized sector workers, guaranteeing a minimum monthly pension between ₹1,000 and ₹5,000 after the age of 60.
8. **Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)**
   - *Purpose:* Provides loans up to ₹10 lakhs to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises.
9. **e-Shram Portal Registration**
   - *Purpose:* Creation of a National Database of Unorganized Workers (NDUW) for providing social security benefits to migrant workers, construction workers, and gig workers.
10. **Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)**
    - *Purpose:* Aims to safeguard the health of women & children by providing free LPG connections to families living below the poverty line (BPL).

## Most Common Government Certificates citizens Apply For

1. **Aadhaar Card** (New enrollment, address updates, mobile linking)
2. **Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card** (New applications and corrections)
3. **Income Certificate** (Crucial for EWS quotas, scholarships, and scheme eligibility)
4. **Caste Certificate** (SC/ST/OBC certification for education and employment reservations)
5. **Domicile / Resident Certificate** (Proof of permanent residence in a specific state)
6. **Birth and Death Certificates** (Fundamental civic registrations)
7. **Driving License & Learner's License** (RTO services via Sarathi Parivahan)
8. **Voter ID Card (EPIC)** (Registration, address updates, linking with Aadhaar)

## Most Common Tax & Business Services

1. **Income Tax Return (ITR) Filing** (Particularly ITR-1 for salaried individuals and ITR-4 for presumptive taxation)
2. **Goods and Services Tax (GST) Registration & Return Filing** (For MSMEs and traders)
3. **Udyam Registration** (MSME registration for business recognition and scheme subsidies)
4. **Property Tax Payment** (Municipal corporation tax payments)
5. **EPF (Employee Provident Fund) Services** (UAN activation, KYC update, claim withdrawals)
